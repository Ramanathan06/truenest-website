Our Philosophy

Three pillars of the quiet life.

Every TrueNest residence is shaped by these principles — written into the land before the first stone is laid.

01

Biophilic Design

Architecture that follows the contour of the ridge — courtyards, breezeways, and orchards that invite the landscape inside.

02

Sited for Stillness

Sunset-facing, altitude-blessed, and oriented to the long western dusk. Only 76 plots, set into 16,800 sq ft of forested land.

03

Natural Materiality

Lime plaster, kota stone, reclaimed teak. The art of subtraction — focusing on light, volume, and the patina that ages with grace.

The Art of Subtraction

In a city that never stops, we provide the silence.

"We remove the unnecessary — focusing on light, volume, and natural materiality to create spaces that breathe."

TrueNest is a boutique developer of biophilic residences at the intersection of nature and architecture. Five founders, one philosophy, and the slow conviction that home is not a place but a posture.

Light as Material

South-aspect glazing, deep eaves, and clerestory openings tuned to the path of the sun.

Forest as Floor Plan

Existing canopy preserved; circulation traced around mature trees rather than through them.

Living Water

Rainwater harvesting and on-site greywater treatment — every drop returned to the land.

Our Process

From first walk to first key.

01

Site Walk

A morning on the ridge — meet the founders, walk the contour, watch the sun move across the orchard.

02

Plot Selection

Choose your orientation — sunset variant, orchard variant, or the rare upper-ridge horizon suite.

03

Reservation

A modest registration; full documentation, RERA filings, and a slow, transparent paper trail.

04

Possession

Quiet handover by 2026 — followed by a residents' supper at the orchard pavilion.
