# TrueNest Website — Plan Summary

_For your review · 2026-05-19_

---

## What we're building

A cinematic, lead-generating website for TrueNest that does three things well:

1. **Opens with impact** — a 4K hero video that plays smoothly (no buffering), then settles
   into a live, interactive banner for your ongoing project (Retreat 76).
2. **Keeps visitors on the project** — rich individual pages for every project with photos,
   details, and a virtual feel, designed to hold attention and drive enquiries.
3. **Captures every lead** — visitors give their phone + email to unlock the brochure, which
   opens as an elegant page-turning **flipbook**. Every lead is stored and emailed to you.

You'll also get a **simple admin panel** to add projects, update content, swap photos, and
view/download all your leads — no developer needed for day-to-day updates.

---

## How it works (in plain terms)

- The site moves from a basic static page to a proper **managed website** with a database.
- Everything you'd want to change — projects, photos, text, brochures — is editable by you
  through a private login.
- Every brochure download and enquiry becomes a lead you can see, filter, and follow up on.
- It's fast and secure, hosted on professional infrastructure with your own domain.

---

## What it looks like

We've designed three style options for the look and feel. Our recommendation is the
**cinematic luxury option (Sample 3)** — it best showcases the ongoing project and keeps
visitors engaged. Final choice is yours.

The look stays consistent with your brand: deep, refined tones with natural green and copper
accents — understated luxury, not generic.

## The pages

- **Home** — hero video → Retreat 76 banner → company vision → all projects → enquiry
- **Each project** — full details, photo gallery, gated brochure flipbook, enquiry button
- **Story / About** — your philosophy and the people behind TrueNest
- **Projects overview, Testimonials, Careers, Contact**
- **Admin panel** — your private dashboard for content + leads
- Social links in the footer of every page
